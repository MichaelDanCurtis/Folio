# Fixture corpus

## Existing fixtures

The 53 files in `test/fixtures` are **generated by Folio's own scripts** (`scripts/verification/create_fixtures.py` and the per-feature scripts beside it). None was created or saved by Word or Excel. They exercise Folio's importers and exporters against the OOXML specification and against independent readers (openpyxl, python-docx, the Open XML SDK schema validator). They don't prove what Office produces or accepts (ROADMAP §2).
